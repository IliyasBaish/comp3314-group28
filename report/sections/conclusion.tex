\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Label propagation is a compact argument: normalise an RBF graph symmetrically and
the diffusion becomes a contraction whose fixed point is also the unique minimiser
of a smoothness energy with a clamping term. We re-derived both steps and checked
them numerically rather than trusting the printed algebra, which paid for itself
once, and our reproduction behaves as advertised ($0.45\%$ against $22.36\%$ for
1-NN, ten fixed trials) until the bandwidth is loosened. The open problem is not
the derivation, which holds; it is selection.
